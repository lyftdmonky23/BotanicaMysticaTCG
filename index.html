Stage 5: Blend #4 — "Awakening the Primordial" (Apex Tier)

Burn 2x Thornheart Wardens (requires 180 Seeds total) to claim 1x Sylvan Primordial (Mythic Tier).

🔒 Global Hard Cap: Strictly limited to 75 total Sylvan Primordial mints. Once 75 exist, no more can ever be created (requiring a total ecosystem burn of 13,500 Whispering Seeds)!
