The Complete Evolution Cycle

1

Stage 1: Primary Harvest — Whispering Seed

Collect base-tier Whispering Seeds (0.25 WAX entry price). These seeds serve as the infinite-supply raw reagent that powers the entire ecosystem.

2

Stage 2: Blend #1 — "The First Awakening"

Burn 10x Whispering Seeds on MintyBlocks to synthesize 1x Whispering Sprout (Common Tier).

3

Stage 3: Blend #2 — "The Verdant Weave"

Burn 3x Whispering Sprouts (requires 30 Seeds total) to synthesize 1x Briarweave Flora (Uncommon Tier).

4

Stage 4: Blend #3 — "Rise of the Warden"

Burn 3x Briarweave Floras (requires 90 Seeds total) to synthesize 1x Thornheart Warden (Rare Tier).

5

Stage 5: Blend #4 — "Awakening the Primordial" (Apex Tier)

Burn 2x Thornheart Wardens (requires 180 Seeds total) to claim 1x Sylvan Primordial (Mythic Tier).

🔒 Global Hard Cap: Strictly limited to 75 total Sylvan Primordial mints. Once 75 exist, no more can ever be created!
