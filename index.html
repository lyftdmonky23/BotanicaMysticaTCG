🌿 Game Overview

Botanica Mystica combines strategic deflationary mechanics with high-fantasy digital artwork. Collect base-tier Whispering Seeds through drops or secondary markets, then burn them in MintyBlocks synthesis recipes to forge rare, powerful forest guardians.

🍃 The 5-Stage Evolution Cycle

Base Reagent

The raw, foundational magical seed housing dormant elemental forest energy. Serves as the core reagent for all blending recipes.
